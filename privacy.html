Privacy Policy for the game "Shambledown"

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Shambledown is a mobile game for Android and iOS. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no purchases, no analytics or advertising software and no automatic crash reporting. Data leaves your device only when you use the features described below.

2. Data on your device

The game stores on your device: your settings, your local leaderboards, the player name you chose, a randomly generated device code (see section 4) and music you pick from your files under "My Radio" (it is copied into the game's storage). These data stay on the device and are not sent to us.

If your operating system's backup is on (Google backup or iCloud backup), your operating system backs these game data up into your Google or Apple account. Google or Apple are responsible for that; you can switch the backup off in your device's settings.

3. World ranking (only with your consent)

After your first game in Highscore or Time Attack mode the game asks whether your results should go to the world ranking. Until then the world ranking is off. If you agree, the game sends the following to our server after every such game:

  • your player name (in co-op also your partner's name, if both of you agreed),
  • the result: points, wave reached, kills, playing time, game mode and room,
  • the game's version,
  • the random device code (section 4).

The purpose is to place your results in the ranking and show them there. In the ranking every player sees player name, partner name, points, wave and rank. So choose a player name that does not identify you; a real name is not needed.

While the world ranking is on, the game checks with the server as you type a player name whether the name is still free and reserves it for your device. A name whose device has sent nothing for twelve months becomes free again.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time under OPTIONS → MORE → WORLD RANKING; from then on nothing is sent. You delete your entries under SCORES → WORLD → MY DATA → "Forget me"; this deletes every entry of your device on the server.

Retention: until you delete your entries. Entries that break the game's rules (such as impossible results or offensive names) may be deleted by us, or the name replaced by "PLAYER".

Transport: encrypted (TLS). The server sees your IP address while it answers but does not store or log it. It uses it only while answering and for a short-lived in-memory limit on requests per address.

4. Device code

On first contact with the world ranking the game generates a random code (16 characters). From it the game derives a device id and a secret. The id ties your entries to your device, the secret makes sure only your device can change or delete them. The code holds no information about you or your device. You can view it under MY DATA and take it over to another device.

5. Co-op in the same Wi-Fi

In local co-op the two devices connect to each other directly. The device that opens a room announces it in the local network (room name, player name, version). The devices exchange player names, characters, version and the game data. None of it goes to us or to anyone outside your network.

6. Co-op over the internet (relay server)

For co-op over the internet our relay server forwards the data between the two devices. For that it processes the six-digit room code, the IP addresses and ports of both devices and the game data (including player name, character, game version, the device code, whether the world ranking is on, and the running game state). The server keeps none of it permanently: a room ends two minutes after the last packet, and its data are then gone from memory. Its logs hold only room codes and packet counts, no addresses. This connection is currently not encrypted.

Your partner sees your player name and your character in the game, not your IP address.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the feature you request by opening or joining a room). If you do not use co-op over the internet, no connection to the relay server is made.

7. Server and recipients

World ranking and relay run on a server the controller operates himself in Germany. There are no processors and no disclosure to third parties. We do not transfer data to countries outside the EU.

8. App stores and test programmes

Installation and updates go through Google Play or the Apple App Store and TestFlight. Google or Apple process data there under their own responsibility (such as your store account, device details, crash reports). With TestFlight, Apple may pass crash reports and your feedback on to us if you allow that in TestFlight. Google's and Apple's privacy policies apply.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access to your data, to rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority of your region.

Since we know you only by the random device code, the easiest way to access or delete your data is in the game (SCORES → WORLD → MY DATA). For requests by e-mail please tell us your device code, otherwise we cannot find your entries.

11. Children

The game is not directed at children. For the world ranking, persons under 16 need their parents' consent.
